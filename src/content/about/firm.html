Four attorneys. One direct relationship.

Michael Hakeem and Albert Ellis founded the firm in Stockton in 1985. Today, Hakeem, Ellis & Marengo advises individuals, families, professionals, and businesses throughout the Central Valley and represents clients in California trial courts.

The practice spans business and corporate law, civil disputes and litigation, criminal defense, estate planning, family law, probate, professional license defense, real estate, and restraining orders. That range allows the attorneys to recognize when one legal issue affects another and to build a coordinated strategy.

Broad experience with personal attention

The firm is intentionally small enough for direct attorney involvement while offering the depth of experience associated with a broader practice. Clients work with lawyers who understand the file, explain the real choices, and stay focused on a practical result.

Some matters call for a careful transaction or a durable plan. Others require negotiation, an administrative hearing, or trial. In each setting, the attorneys emphasize preparation, candid evaluation, responsiveness, and efficient use of the client’s resources.

The firm’s commitment

Hakeem, Ellis & Marengo is built around integrity, service, and strong legal work. Its attorneys aim to provide timely advice, take responsibility for the matter entrusted to them, and give clients the information they need to make sound decisions.
