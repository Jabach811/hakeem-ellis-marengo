Experience

Albert M. Ellis is a Central Valley trial attorney with decades of experience in civil and criminal cases. He has resolved matters through jury trials, court trials, arbitration, mediation, and negotiated settlement, giving clients a practical view of both courtroom risk and opportunities for resolution.

His work includes criminal defense, family law, restraining orders, business and employment litigation, professional license defense before state disciplinary boards, attorney discipline matters before the State Bar of California, and student-discipline cases involving civil-rights issues.

Education and admissions

Mr. Ellis was admitted to the State Bar of California in 1978. He is also admitted to the United States District Court for the Eastern District of California and the Supreme Court of the United States. He earned an honors bachelor’s degree from the University of California, Berkeley in 1975 and his law degree from Pepperdine University in 1977. He served as an editor of the Clinical Law Journal during 1977 and 1978.

Professional service and practice focus

Mr. Ellis is a member of the San Joaquin County Bar Association and the State Bar of California. His practice focuses on trials and dispute resolution, criminal defense, family matters, restraining orders, business and employment litigation, and the defense of licensed professionals.
