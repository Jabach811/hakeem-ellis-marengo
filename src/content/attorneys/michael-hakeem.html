Experience

Michael D. Hakeem has more than five decades of experience in real estate and civil law. He represents corporate entities and advises on real estate acquisition and development, environmental compliance, land use, contracts, deeds, construction agreements, and other transactional matters.

Mr. Hakeem has advised many of the Central Valley’s largest corporations and regularly appears before county boards of supervisors, local planning commissions, and state agencies. He is also an experienced negotiator who has resolved many matters through settlement when that serves the client better than litigation.

Education and admissions

Mr. Hakeem was admitted to the State Bar of California in 1973 and is admitted to the United States District Courts for the Central and Eastern Districts of California. He earned his bachelor’s degree from the University of San Francisco in 1968 and his law degree from Santa Clara University in 1972. He served as president of Phi Alpha Delta from 1971 to 1972.

Professional service and practice focus

Mr. Hakeem has served the San Joaquin County Bar Association as a member of its Board of Governors, on its Client Relations Committee, and as bar president from 1985 to 1986. His professional memberships have included the State Bar of California, California Trial Lawyers Association, and San Joaquin County Trial Lawyers.

His reported case includes California Aviation Council v. County of Amador (FHA Properties, Inc.), 200 Cal.App.3d 337 (1998). His practice focuses on real estate law, real property acquisition and development, land-use entitlements, environmental compliance, transactions, and related civil matters.
