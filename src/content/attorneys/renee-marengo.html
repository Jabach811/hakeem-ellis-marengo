Experience

Renee M. Marengo advises clients forming corporations, partnerships, limited liability companies, nonprofit corporations, and other California business entities. She helps owners choose and document a structure that reflects management, ownership, liability, and long-term plans.

Ms. Marengo also counsels corporations and individuals on assets and liabilities, estate planning, trusts, probate, and wealth-management strategies. Her combined business and estate experience is especially useful when a closely held company, real property, or succession plan crosses between personal and corporate decisions.

Education and admissions

Ms. Marengo was admitted to the State Bar of California in 1984. She earned her bachelor’s degree from the University of California, San Diego in 1980 and her law degree from the University of San Francisco in 1984.

Professional service and practice focus

Her professional memberships have included the San Joaquin County Bar Association, American Bar Association, State Bar of California, and San Joaquin County Women Lawyers, where she served as president from 1985 to 1986.

Her practice focuses on general corporate law, business formation and governance, estate planning, trusts, probate, and related asset-planning matters.
