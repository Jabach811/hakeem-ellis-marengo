Experience

Stephen J. Baker handles civil and criminal litigation, including trials and appeals. His work includes civil disputes, restraining orders, real property and partition actions, eminent domain, breach of contract, prenuptial agreements, family law, and criminal defense.

Before joining private practice, Mr. Baker served as a Former Deputy District Attorney at the San Joaquin County District Attorney’s Office. That experience informs his assessment of criminal charges, evidence, courtroom procedure, and case resolution.

Education and admissions

Mr. Baker was admitted to the State Bar of California in 2023. He earned an honors bachelor’s degree from Sonoma State University and graduated magna cum laude from Humphreys University Lawrence Drivon School of Law in 2022.

Practice focus

His practice focuses on civil litigation, criminal defense, family law, real-property disputes, partition and eminent-domain matters, contract claims, appeals, and restraining-order proceedings.
