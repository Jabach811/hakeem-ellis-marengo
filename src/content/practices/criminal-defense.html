Early representation matters

A criminal investigation or charge can affect freedom, employment, professional licensing, family, and reputation. Early legal advice can help a person understand what to say, what not to say, what evidence may exist, and which deadlines require immediate action. The firm provides direct representation from investigation and arraignment through negotiation, motions, trial, and sentencing when necessary.

Every case is different. The attorneys examine the alleged conduct, the government’s evidence, search and seizure issues, witness accounts, prior history, and the personal consequences of each possible outcome.

Charges the firm handles

Representation includes misdemeanor and felony matters, driving-under-the-influence charges, drug offenses, theft and other property crimes, weapons allegations, and federal criminal matters. The firm also advises professionals and business owners when a criminal case creates licensing, employment, or regulatory concerns.

The defense process

Effective defense begins with investigation and a careful review of the record. Counsel may challenge the legality of a stop, search, statement, identification, or charging decision; develop evidence that provides context or contradicts the accusation; and evaluate negotiated resolutions against the risks of trial.

Clients receive a plain explanation of court dates, likely next steps, available defenses, and the direct and collateral consequences of a plea or conviction.

Sentencing and post-conviction support

When a case reaches sentencing, preparation can affect the court’s understanding of the person and the available alternatives. The firm develops relevant mitigation and advocates for a proportionate result. It also assists with appropriate post-conviction matters, including questions about records and the effect of a case on a professional license.
