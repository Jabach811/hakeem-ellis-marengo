Divorce and legal separation

Family law decisions affect finances, children, housing, and daily life. The firm guides clients through divorce and legal separation with a focus on clear priorities and durable solutions. Representation may include property characterization and division, support, business interests, retirement assets, debt, and the procedural steps needed to complete the case.

Some families can resolve most issues through negotiation or mediation. Others require temporary orders, discovery, expert analysis, or trial. The attorneys help clients understand the choices and prepare for the path the case requires.

Child custody and visitation

Custody and parenting-time decisions are guided by the child’s best interests. The firm helps parents develop workable schedules, address decision-making responsibilities, prepare for mediation, and present disputed issues to the court. It also handles requests to modify existing orders when circumstances materially change.

When safety concerns, relocation, substance use, domestic violence, or high conflict affects a parenting plan, the matter may require additional evidence and careful coordination with related proceedings.

Child and spousal support

Support calculations depend on financial information, parenting time, income, and other statutory factors. The attorneys assist with initial orders, modification requests, enforcement, and disputes involving self-employment or incomplete financial disclosure. The aim is an accurate presentation of the facts and a clear understanding of how an order may change over time.

Adoption and marital agreements

The firm assists with adoption matters and with agreements made before or during marriage. Prenuptial and postnuptial agreements can define property rights and expectations, but they require thoughtful disclosure, independent advice, and careful drafting. Counsel helps clients create documents suited to their circumstances rather than relying on generic forms.
