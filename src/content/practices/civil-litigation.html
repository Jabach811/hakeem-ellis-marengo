Disputes the firm handles

The firm represents plaintiffs and defendants in civil litigation involving businesses, contracts, real property, construction, professional relationships, and other contested rights. Some matters begin with a demand letter or urgent request for relief; others arrive after months of unsuccessful negotiation. In either setting, the attorneys work to identify the decisive facts and the most useful remedy.

Litigation strategy is tailored to the value, urgency, and complexity of the dispute. A focused case may call for a narrow and efficient approach, while a high-stakes matter may require extensive document review, expert analysis, and trial preparation.

Preparation from the beginning

Strong cases are built through disciplined preparation. The firm evaluates claims and defenses, preserves evidence, interviews witnesses, reviews contracts and records, and develops a realistic timeline. Once a lawsuit is filed, the attorneys handle pleadings, discovery, depositions, motions, hearings, and other court requirements.

Clients are kept informed about significant developments and the choices that affect cost, timing, and outcome.

Negotiation and mediation

A negotiated resolution can preserve resources and give the parties more control than a court judgment. The firm approaches settlement from a position grounded in the law and the evidence, not as an afterthought. It helps clients evaluate proposals, prepare for mediation, and document enforceable terms when an agreement is reached.

Hearings and trial

When a fair resolution is not available, the firm is prepared to present the case in court. Trial work connects legal arguments with a clear factual story and reliable proof. The attorneys also advise on post-trial issues and appeals when those steps are appropriate.
