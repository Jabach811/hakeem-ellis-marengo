Understanding protective orders

Restraining orders can address safety, contact, residence, employment, firearms, and other important rights on a very short timetable. The firm represents people seeking protection and people responding to requested orders. Counsel helps identify the correct type of proceeding, organize admissible evidence, prepare declarations and witnesses, and present the matter at hearing.

Because temporary orders may be issued before both sides are heard, it is important to read every document carefully and comply with any order currently in effect while preparing a response.

Domestic violence orders

Domestic violence restraining-order matters can involve spouses, former partners, co-parents, relatives, and people who live or have lived together. The court may consider allegations of physical harm, threats, harassment, stalking, coercive control, or other abuse. These proceedings can overlap with custody, divorce, support, and criminal cases, requiring a coordinated strategy.

Elder and dependent-adult abuse

Protective orders may be available when an elder or dependent adult faces physical abuse, neglect, abandonment, isolation, abduction, or financial abuse. The firm helps clients assess standing, document the alleged conduct, and address related questions involving family, property, caregiving, or estate administration.

Civil harassment and workplace violence

Civil harassment orders generally address conduct outside the close relationships covered by domestic violence law. Employers may also seek workplace-violence orders to protect an employee from credible threats or violence. Each process has its own legal standards and proof requirements.

Representation for both sides

The consequences of an order can be significant, including limits on contact and movement, firearm restrictions, and effects on related cases or employment. Whether seeking or opposing protection, clients need a clear account of the facts, relevant records, and focused hearing preparation. The firm explains the order, the process, and the practical next steps.
