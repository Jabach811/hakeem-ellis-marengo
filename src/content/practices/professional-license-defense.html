Defense for licensed professionals

A professional license represents years of education, training, and work. An application issue, complaint, investigation, or accusation can threaten both livelihood and reputation. The firm represents licensed professionals before California boards and agencies and helps clients understand the administrative process from the first inquiry through hearing and review.

Matters may involve health care professionals, contractors, real estate professionals, educators, and others whose work depends on state authorization. Because each licensing body has its own rules and procedures, early attention to notices and response dates is important.

Investigations, accusations, and hearings

Representation may begin with a complaint, interview request, subpoena, or board investigation. The attorneys help clients gather records, prepare responses, evaluate allegations, and communicate with investigators. If an agency files a formal accusation, the firm can handle settlement discussions, discovery, administrative hearings, and related court review.

The strategy considers not only the immediate allegation but also the professional’s record, current practice, remediation, and the practical effect of proposed discipline.

Applications and criminal-case overlap

License applications and renewals may require disclosure of criminal cases, prior discipline, or other events. The firm advises on accurate disclosures and the supporting evidence that may help an agency evaluate rehabilitation and present fitness. When a criminal matter is active, licensing and defense strategies should be coordinated so that a statement in one proceeding does not create avoidable problems in another.

Protecting livelihood and reputation

Administrative cases can move differently from ordinary court cases, but the stakes are deeply personal. The firm provides direct advice about response options, likely agency concerns, settlement terms, hearing risk, and the steps that may support a stronger outcome.
