Transactions and development

Real estate matters combine contracts, financing, land-use rules, physical conditions, and long-term business decisions. The firm represents owners, buyers, sellers, developers, landlords, tenants, and businesses in transactions involving commercial and residential property. Work may include purchase and sale agreements, due diligence, leases, title issues, easements, development arrangements, and closing questions.

Early legal review can clarify contingencies, disclosure duties, timing, remedies, and the allocation of costs before the parties become committed to a deal.

Property and construction disputes

Disputes can arise from boundaries, access, ownership, contracts, construction performance, leases, or competing uses of property. The attorneys evaluate the governing documents, recorded interests, project history, and practical value of the property to help clients choose a proportionate strategy.

The firm represents clients in negotiations and litigation involving easements, ownership claims, contract performance, commercial tenancies, development obligations, and construction-related issues.

Land use and entitlements

Development may require permits, approvals, environmental review, zoning analysis, or work with public agencies. The firm advises on land-use questions and helps clients understand the legal framework affecting a proposed use or project. Counsel can assist with applications, conditions, negotiations, administrative proceedings, and related disputes.

Choosing a resolution path

Not every real estate problem belongs in court. Depending on the facts, a negotiated amendment, purchase, access agreement, mediation, administrative remedy, or focused litigation may provide the best result. The firm explains the available paths, likely costs, and business consequences so the client can make an informed decision.
